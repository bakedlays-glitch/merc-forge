Stable Diffusion img2img + ControlNet workflow for animation frames.

Settings (recommended baseline; tune per portrait):
- Denoising strength: 0.35-0.50 (lower preserves identity better)
- CFG scale: 7
- Sampler: DPM++ 2M Karras
- Steps: 30
- ControlNet: OpenPose or Reference-Only at 0.7-0.9 weight to lock face
- Use a face-region mask in the inpaint tab for surgical changes

Per-frame prompt suffix (append to your subject description):

ANIM_EYE_1: half-closed eyes, eyelids 50% lowered covering iris,
sleepy half-blink expression, identical lighting and skin

ANIM_EYE_2: fully closed eyes, eyelids touching, peaceful eyes shut,
identical lighting and skin, no change to mouth or face structure

ANIM_MOUTH_1: lips slightly parted, 1-2mm gap between lips, beginning
to speak, identical eyes and forehead

ANIM_MOUTH_2: mouth wide open speaking loudly, visible teeth, mid-speech,
identical eyes and hair

ANIM_MOUTH_3: mouth medium open, neutral talking, slight tooth row,
identical eyes and skin

Negative prompt suggestions:
closed eyes (for mouth frames), open eyes (for blink frames), different
face, different person, different hairstyle, smiling, smirk, makeup change

After generation, ensure all 5 outputs are 1024×1024 PNG. Resize if needed
(LANCZOS for quality). Drag back into Merc Wizard.
